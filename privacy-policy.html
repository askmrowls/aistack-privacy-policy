AI Stack

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2, 2026

AI Stack respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and what choices users have.

1. Overview

AI Stack is a learning game that helps users explore AI tools and workflows through interactive missions, scoring, and progress tracking.

At its current stage, the app is designed to work primarily on-device. Based on the version in this project folder, user progress is stored locally in the browser or app on the user's device.

2. Information We Collect

Information stored locally on your device

  • Game progress
  • Completed missions
  • Scores and grades
  • XP, streaks, and badges
  • Unlocked chapters
  • Daily challenge completion state
  • Gameplay-related preferences

This information is stored locally so the app can remember progress between sessions.

Information not currently collected directly

In the current version of the app in this folder, AI Stack does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, payment card information, or precise location.

3. Analytics and Third-Party Services

The current local version in this folder does not include a live analytics or advertising backend by default.

However, future app store or production versions of AI Stack may use third-party services such as analytics, crash reporting, in-app purchase platforms, push notification services, or affiliate links to third-party AI tools.

If those services are added, they may collect limited technical data such as device type, operating system, app version, usage events, purchase status, and referral or link-click information.

4. How We Use Information

  • Save and restore user progress
  • Improve gameplay and app performance
  • Understand which features are most useful
  • Support purchases or subscriptions
  • Measure app stability and engagement
  • Provide access to optional external tool links

5. Data Sharing

AI Stack does not sell personal information.

If third-party tools are added in a production release, limited data may be shared only as needed to operate services such as analytics, app store billing, crash reporting, notifications, or affiliate referral tracking.

6. Data Retention

For the current version of the app, gameplay data stored locally remains on the user's device until the user clears browser or app storage, removes the app, or resets progress.

7. Children’s Privacy

AI Stack is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by the app. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. External Links

AI Stack may include links to third-party websites, tools, or services. If you follow those links, you will be subject to the privacy practices of those third parties.

10. Your Choices

Users can control local app data by clearing browser storage, uninstalling the app, or using any in-app reset-progress feature if available.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If it changes, the updated version should be posted with a new effective date.
